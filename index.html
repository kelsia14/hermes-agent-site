Hermes Agent

A personal, self-hosted automation agent for a single user's own Google account.

What this application is

Hermes Agent is a private automation tool that runs on its owner's own infrastructure. It is used by one person — its owner — to automate everyday tasks against that same person's Google account. It is not a commercial product, it has no other users, and it does not offer sign-up to the public.

What it does

  • Reads and triages incoming email, and drafts or sends replies on the owner's behalf when asked.
  • Extracts structured data from receipts and invoices (for example fuel invoices) and books them into the owner's expense spreadsheet.
  • Reads and creates calendar events, and searches or organises files in the owner's Drive.
  • Looks up the owner's own contacts when resolving who sent a message.

Every operation is performed only after the owner has explicitly authorised this application through Google's standard OAuth 2.0 consent flow, and only within the scopes granted there.
