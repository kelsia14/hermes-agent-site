Privacy Policy

Hermes Agent — personal instance. Effective 13 October 2026.

This policy explains how Hermes Agent (the "Application") accesses, uses and stores information from a Google Account. The Application is a personal, self-hosted automation tool with a single user: the owner of the Google Account that authorises it. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

1. Who is responsible

The Application is operated by its owner for personal use only. Contact: open an issue in this repository.

2. What Google data is accessed

Only after you authorise the Application through Google's OAuth 2.0 consent screen, and only within the scopes you grant, the Application may access:

  • Gmail — read messages, modify labels, and send messages (scopes gmail.readonly, gmail.modify, gmail.send).
  • Google Calendar — read, create and manage events (scope calendar).
  • Google Drive — search, read, create and organise files (scope drive).
  • Google Sheets — read and write spreadsheet rows (scope spreadsheets).
  • Google Docs — read and edit documents (scope documents).
  • Contacts — read-only, to resolve the people who appear in your email (scope contacts.readonly).

The Application does not request or access any Google data beyond these scopes.

3. How the data is used

Data is used solely to carry out the tasks you ask the Application to perform for you, such as:

  • summarising, triaging, labelling and searching your email;
  • drafting or sending replies that you request;
  • extracting invoice and receipt details and recording them in your expense spreadsheet;
  • reading, creating or managing calendar events;
  • finding, reading or organising files in your Drive.

The Application does not use Google data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, lending, or any purpose unrelated to the tasks above.

4. Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens are stored on infrastructure controlled by the owner, in files readable only by the owner's user account, and are refreshed programmatically.
  • Message and file content is processed on that same infrastructure. It is not copied into any public location and is not stored in any third-party account.
  • Access is protected by the owner's own operating-system and network security controls.

5. Automated processing by a language model

To extract structured fields from documents (for example, the totals and dates on an invoice), the text of the relevant document may be sent to a third-party large language model provider through its commercial API, strictly for that one processing step. That processing is governed by the provider's API terms; the Application's owner has instructed that the data is not used to train models. No Google data is transferred to any other third party.

6. Sharing

The Application does not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties, other than the processing step described in section 5. It does not transfer Google user data to advertisers, data brokers, or any party for purposes unrelated to the tasks you request.

7. Retention and deletion

Data extracted from your Google account is retained only as long as it is useful for the task it was created for — for example, a booked expense row stays in your spreadsheet until you delete it. To delete data, delete it at source in your Google Account. To have any locally cached copy removed, use the contact method in section 1.

8. Limited Use disclosure

Hermes Agent's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Revoking access

You can revoke the Application's access at any time:

  • Visit your Google Account's third-party connections page and remove Hermes Agent's access, or
  • revoke its tokens directly through the Application's own authorisation tooling.

After revocation the Application can no longer access your Google data.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Application. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly.

11. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data: open an issue in this repository
